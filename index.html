Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 9, 2026

Our approach

Audition Tracker is a personal organizational tool for actors. You can use it without creating an account. RoleCaddy does not operate a developer-controlled server to store your casting records.

Information you provide

You may enter project and role names, casting status, deadlines, audition and callback details, production and shooting dates, compensation, locations, contacts, links, notes and other information. You may also import files such as scripts or audition materials. You choose what to enter; please avoid storing information you do not have permission to keep.

Where your information is stored

Casting records and app preferences are saved on your device using iOS local storage (UserDefaults). Imported files are copied into the app’s local Documents directory. Audition Tracker does not provide an account-based cloud synchronization service or its own remote database.

How the app uses information

Your information is used to organize and display projects, calendar entries, deadlines, casting progress and related details. When reminders are enabled, relevant project names and event details are used to schedule local notifications through iOS. Notifications are optional and can be disabled globally, for individual projects or in iOS Settings.

Apple Maps and external services

When you search for a shooting location, the search is processed using Apple MapKit and Apple Maps services. The search terms you submit may be handled by Apple under its own privacy terms. The app saves selected location information, including place names, addresses and coordinates, as part of your local casting record. If you choose to open external links or share a file, the destination app or service may process information under its own policies.

Advertising and analytics

The current app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, developer-operated analytics services or cross-app tracking features. It does not sell casting records to advertisers. Apple may independently process diagnostic or App Store information under its own policies and your device settings.

Access, editing and deletion

You can review, update and delete casting records within the app. Imported attachments may remain in local app storage after an individual casting is deleted; deleting the app removes its app-managed local storage in the normal iOS deletion process. If you want to erase all app-managed data, remove the app from your device after saving anything you wish to keep. Deleting the app is not the same as removing it from the Home Screen.

Data retention and backups

Local information remains on your device until you change or delete it, remove the app, or iOS removes app data. The app does not currently offer built-in cloud sync or data export. Device-level backups, if enabled, are controlled by iOS and your Apple account settings; do not assume the app provides an independent backup.

Security

Audition Tracker relies on the protections provided by iOS for app data and device access. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with a passcode and avoid storing unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Children and other people’s information

Audition Tracker is intended as a general organizational tool and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you record details about casting contacts or other people, you are responsible for having an appropriate reason to keep and use those details.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date is shown above. For privacy questions or requests, contact RoleCaddy at rolecaddy.dev@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current app features. Apple’s own services and the App Store are subject to Apple’s separate terms and privacy practices.
